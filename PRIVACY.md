# HTML Layers Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

HTML Layers does not collect, store or share any personal or browsing data.

The extension reads the structure of the page you are viewing (headings, landmarks, lists and their visibility) only when you click its icon or use its shortcut. This analysis happens entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server, and the extension uses no analytics or tracking.

If you turn on "Allow on all sites", the extension can keep running when you switch tabs or reload, without a new click. This does not change how data is handled: everything stays in your browser. You can remove this permission at any time in chrome://extensions.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/inessadl/html-layers/issues
